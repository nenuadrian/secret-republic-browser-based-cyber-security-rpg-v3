## Starting out

New players register by choosing one of **six zones**. They get a random starting node on the Grid and are
placed in their zone's **beginner organization**. An 18-step **tutorial**, with rewards at each step, walks
through the main systems. Players can skip steps.

Tutorial steps: Step numero zero · Me haz rewardz · Friends with benefits · A server? · Missions ·
All for one and one for party · the node maze · dark side of the grid · Skills · Abs · Server skills and
apps · Data Points · Jobs · Training · Forumsy · Organizations · Easy peasy · My Computer Science Degree.

## Missions, jobs and training

- **Missions** are played in a terminal: scan hosts, crack services, read mail, run programs and query
  databases. See the [mission console](../missions/).
- **Jobs** give players a designed mission that matches their level, for a reward, on a cooldown.
- **Training** has captchas and other mini-games to complete regularly for rewards.
- **Parties** let players team up for multi-player missions.

## Skills and abilities

**16 skills** take points earned by levelling up: Dynamic Programming, Network protection, Vulnerability
analysis, Intrusion detection, Tracing prevention, Firewall Cracking, Memory Analysis, Task Automation,
Data Processing, Cardinal Operating System, Network Sniffing, Databases, Data Points Mining, Node
Initialization Success Rate (NISR), Hardware repair and Software repair.

**8 abilities** level up over time: Encryption, Typing speed, Discretion, The Unseen Hand, SQL Injection,
Researching, IP spoofing and SSL Decryption.

## Servers, hardware and software

Players build **servers** and upgrade their hardware: motherboard, RAM, HDD and power source. **Software**
runs on a server and uses its resources. Servers produce **Data Points**, which the
[resources cron job](../../getting-started/cron-jobs/) mines over time. The workbench and storage hold
components and items.

## The Grid, zones and attacks

The world is split into **6 zones**, each with many clusters of nodes. Every player owns a main node and can
initialize empty nodes or take nodes from others. **Damage** and **spy attacks** can be launched between
nodes; an attack **simulator** previews the outcome. Zones have their own wars and leadership.

## Organizations and wars

Player-owned guilds with custom ranks and permissions, membership applications, internal forums and
organization missions. Organizations can declare **wars** on each other and are ranked separately.

## The hackdown

A weekly competition built on missions and arena battles, run by the
[hackdown cron jobs](../../getting-started/cron-jobs/), with rankings for players, organizations and zones.

## Economy and rewards

- **Money** and **Data Points** come from missions, jobs, servers and rewards.
- **Alpha Coins** are the premium currency, with an in-game shop and coupons.
- The **rewards** system bundles several items into one reward that the player accepts. Players can review
  their reward history.
- **Achievements** and **level rewards** are managed in the admin panel.

## Community

Forums (including private organization forums), player **blogs**, threaded **conversations**, **friends**,
**referrals**, a **notes** page, **search**, **rankings** and world statistics (**theWorld**).

## Audio AI

Pre-recorded voice cues ("Eve") play when certain actions happen. Players can mute them.
